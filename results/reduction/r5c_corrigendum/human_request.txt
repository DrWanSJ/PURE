10. CORRECTION #8 — RAPID-EQUILIBRIUM STATE REDUCTION DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY PRESERVE MICROSCOPIC GROSS FLUXES
============================================================

This is the main accounting correction.

For one selected reversible fast pair:

    forward rate  v+
    reverse rate  v-

define:

    j = v+ - v-

The slow state dynamics depend on j through stoichiometry.

The gross directed extents are:

    xi+ = integral v+ dt
    xi- = integral v- dt

These are different observables.

Under the singular scaling:

    v+^eta ~ O(1/eta)
    v-^eta ~ O(1/eta)

while:

    j^eta = v+^eta - v-^eta

may remain O(1).

Therefore as eta -> 0:

    individual gross forward/reverse turnover can become arbitrarily large

while:

    their net stoichiometric effect remains finite.

Hence:

    state convergence does NOT imply
    gross directed extent convergence with the same conditioning.

Document the numerical-cancellation consequence.

Do not label it as a software bug unless evidence specifically supports that.
