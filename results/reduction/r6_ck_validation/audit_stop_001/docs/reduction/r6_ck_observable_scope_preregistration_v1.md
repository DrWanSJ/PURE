1. Scientific use: explain mRNA-directed fMGG translation, resource limitations, chemistry and functional occupancy. The legacy GFP model card is a Mavelli benchmark, not the PNAS product definition. PNAS's released product is `Pept0003` (fMet-Gly-Gly), not a mature GFP fluorescence readout.
2. Model states: retain reconstructable protected trajectories, enzyme occupancy, bound resources, all exact SOURCE_GENERAL coordinates and their affine lift. The species information contract permits algebraic representation of Class II-A states; it does not decide flux accuracy.
3. Net accounting: distinguish state inventories, net biochemical conversion, net integrated conversion, exact source conservation and initial redistribution. Product agreement alone is insufficient.
4. Microscopic gross flux: separately audit the need for each CK binding/unbinding rate and each directed cumulative extent. ATP gross production/consumption is not automatically CP binding turnover. Source-provenance retention is not a numerical accuracy certificate.
