## Interpretation

This document records **what information the reduced model is required to retain** before any reaction-level reduction is finalized.

- **I — protected dynamic observable:** the trajectory must remain directly outputtable, although exact conservation may later make it algebraically reconstructed rather than an independent ODE coordinate.
- **II-A — enzyme/catalytic intermediate:** individual catalytic microstate is not a protected output. Algebraic/QSSA/effective reconstruction is allowed only if the corresponding enzyme occupancy and bound resource moieties remain reconstructable. **This classification is not evidence that QSSA has already been validated.**
- **II-B — functional occupancy:** microscopic identity may be lumped, but the declared factor/ribosome/peptide occupancy pool and all relevant moieties must remain reconstructable.
- **III — elongation microstate identity unprotected:** codon-position microstate may disappear, but `R_elong`, elongation flux, peptide progress, tRNA/ribosome occupancy and resource ledgers remain protected.
- **C — cumulative degradation accounting:** terminal non-feedback sink may disappear from the main ODE, but cumulative loss must remain available for material/family accounting.

The `Source module` column is copied from the current repository audit classification. A species may participate in multiple mechanistic modules; that source label does not by itself determine the final reduced representation.

## Human decisions fixed in this version

- `Pept0002` → II-B / peptide-length-2 pool.
- `RS50S_tRNAGlyGCC` → II-B / `R_recycle`.
- Three UAA stop-codon states `elRS70SAUAA0004_Pept0003tRNAGlyGCC{,_RF1,_RF2}` → II-B / `R_term/R_preterm`.
- Free `RS30S`, `RS50S`, and `RS70S` remain Class I.
- Derived observables `AT`, `P_complete`, `a_polymerized`, `R_init`, `R_elong`, `R_term`, and `R_recycle` are not counted as extra source species.
