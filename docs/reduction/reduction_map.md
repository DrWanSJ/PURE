# PNAS 2017 — reduction MAP (Phase 6) — **a map, not a reduction**

> **This document performs NO reduction.** It classifies the 968-reaction
> reference network and proposes *candidate* transformations with their
> consequences. Every non-KEEP row in the companion CSV is
> `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED = true`. The human researcher makes the scientific
> reduction decisions. No reaction is deleted, no state is removed, and no QSSA
> is performed here.

Companion machine-readable table:
`docs/reduction/reduction_decisions.csv` (968 rows, original reaction ids).
Generated by `scripts/build_pnas2017_reduction_map.py` from the SBML audit
artefacts.

### What the labels are **not**

Candidate labels are derived from **SBML structure only** (reaction arity,
reverse-pairing, degradation involvement, free-carrier deltas, particle-number
change). They carry **no** timescale, flux or concentration evidence yet,
because the SI Datasets have not been parsed. A `*_CANDIDATE` tag is therefore a
prompt to investigate, **not** a justified simplification. Do not use "protein
output unchanged" as a reduction criterion.

## Level C — structural reaction families

`reduction_decisions.csv` assigns a `level_C_family` to each reaction from
mechanical features. The dominant structural finding:

- **580 reactions form 290 forward+reverse pairs** — the SBML exports every
  reversible elementary step as two irreversible reactions. Structurally these
  can be **re-combined into 290 reversible reactions** (a representation change,
  not a loss of chemistry). Whether any pair is additionally at
  *fast equilibrium* is a **separate** claim requiring timescale data.
- **388 reactions are irreversible `_degraded`-turnover steps** — these return
  monomers/nucleotides/phosphate to the pools (recycling). They are the *only*
  irreversible reactions in the model, so they are load-bearing for the resource
  ledger and must not be dropped casually.

## Candidate labels — current mechanical tally

| candidate label | count | why the machine proposed it | what it still needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `LUMP_CANDIDATE` | 580 | reaction is half of a reversible pair | confirm pair → single reversible; then test if quasi-equilibrium |
| `DROP_CANDIDATE` | 388 | irreversible degradation/turnover | **must not drop without replacement**; quantify its particle + recycling role, else it is `KEEP` |
| `KEEP` | 0 | — | the structural pass alone cannot justify a KEEP backbone |

> **HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED:** `DROP_CANDIDATE` on degradation is deliberately
> provocative. Dropping a degradation step changes `net_particle_number_change`
> and removes the recycling flux that feeds the amino-acid / nucleotide /
> phosphate pools the ledger tracks. The default scientific stance is that these
> are **KEEP unless the reduction explicitly re-adds a sink/source that
> conserves the ledger.**

### Standard consequence statement (applies to every non-KEEP label)

- **Conservation / moiety:** after reduction the moiety sums (tRNA pool,
  ribosome pool, adenine/guanine nucleotide pools, phosphate) must be
  **re-derived**; a lump that hides a moiety-bearing species breaks them.
- **ATP/GTP accounting:** any transformation that removes a complex carrying an
  ATP/GTP moiety must preserve the net free-carrier turnover or it corrupts the
  energy ledger.
- **PPi/Pi:** PPi appears only from aminoacylation and is cleared by PPiase; Pi
  (PO4) is produced by 33 reactions — collapse of these must keep Σphosphate.
- **tRNA accounting:** 22 aminoacyl-tRNA + 76 tRNA species cycle; merging them
  loses the charged/uncharged split the resource model needs.
- **Osmolarity:** `net_particle_number_change` must be conserved or explicitly
  compensated; 625 reactions raise and 266 lower particle count.
- **Ionic strength:** *not assessable* — charge/protonation undefined in SBML.
- **Unrecoverable observables:** each proposal lists, in the human review doc,
  which intermediate concentrations would no longer be reconstructable.
- **Data to validate:** SI Datasets (esp. per-subsystem time courses S1–S26,
  combined run S27/S28) once parsed.
