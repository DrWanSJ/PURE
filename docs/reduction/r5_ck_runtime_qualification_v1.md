# CK solver-domain qualification, before completed comparisons

The first outer solve stopped after four RHS calls because a BDF internal Newton trial made a zero initial total slightly negative. Its original result and traceback remain under ck/eta_0/outer/result.json. No physical sample or convergence result was produced by this attempt.

The equal-K quadratic expression admits a smooth algebraic extension around the physical domain. The runtime evaluates this same expression on internal solver trial values; it does not clip, project, change parameters or adjust solver tolerances. The public physical-root verifier still rejects negative totals. Accepted-state and reported-state minima are retained and assessed separately; an algebraic extension is not evidence of physical validity outside nonnegative totals. Subsequent attempts are stored in numbered directories instead of replacing failed evidence.

An absolute-total attempt hit the fixed 300000 RHS-call bound. A further compensated-row attempt retains progress/checkpoints and the same bound. This exposed a representational issue: the exact class expresses a near-zero Cr coordinate as the difference of a 50000-scale B total and its initial value. Absolute totals can quantize those small increments, injecting numerical noise at the unchanged 1e-14 absolute solver scale.

Before further completed comparisons, the solver now integrates centered invariant coordinates dz=z−z0. Full reconstruction is x=x0+Xz dz+D(q−q_full0), with compensated row sums; physical totals for binding are z0+dz. This is the same exact affine coordinate map and differential equation. It neither fits initial conditions nor changes canonical data, mathematical dynamics, solver tolerances, the eta sequence, time grid or switch rule. Accepted arrays explicitly identify centered coordinates. The previous attempts remain unchanged. This arithmetic implementation qualification does not establish asymptotic consistency.

Projected RHS rows and source RHS rows are also evaluated with math.fsum, matching the historical stable-direct arithmetic policy. Fast gross cancellation in small derivatives is thereby separated from matrix-dot rounding; stoichiometric coefficients and reaction-rate formulas remain identical. No tolerance or computational bound is increased.

Inspection found that the initial rank selection prioritized source species order, thereby substituting many original R1 retained species with poorly conditioned reconstructed coordinates. The final equivalent fast-invariant basis prioritizes unaffected R1 retained species, followed by the unaffected reconstructed species. This preserves R1's intended chart except where the two binding directions require replacing coordinates. All three dynamic fast totals remain included; full Lf rank239, restricted rank212 and exact identities are unchanged. Prior failed attempts retain their own progress/arrays and are not used in decisive comparisons. Final successful run metadata binds runtime and runner bytes.
